# Store Assets & Release Notes

Place final store screenshots and promotional graphics in this directory before submission.

## iOS Screenshots Required (App Store Connect)
- **6.7" (iPhone 15 Pro Max / 16 Pro Max):** 1290 × 2796 px
- **6.5" (iPhone 14 Plus / 13 Pro Max):** 1284 × 2778 px
- **5.5" (iPhone 8 Plus):** 1242 × 2208 px
- **iPad (12.9" 2nd gen / 6th gen):** 2048 × 2732 px

Tip: You only need the 6.7" screenshots if you support newer devices; Apple will scale down for smaller phones. However, 5.5" and iPad are still separate requirements if you support those form factors.

## Android Screenshots Required (Google Play Console)
- **Phone:** Up to 8 screenshots (16:9 or 9:16), min 320 px, max 3840 px
- **7" tablet:** Up to 8 screenshots
- **10" tablet:** Up to 8 screenshots

## Feature Graphic (Google Play only)
- 1024 × 500 px JPEG or PNG

## App Preview Video (Optional)
- iOS: 15–30 seconds, device-specific resolutions
- Android: 30 seconds–2 minutes, 720p or 1080p
