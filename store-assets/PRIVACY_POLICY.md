# Privacy Policy for Dosed Tracker

**Effective Date:** June 10, 2026

**Last Updated:** June 10, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Dosed Tracker (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you use the Dosed Tracker mobile application (the “App”).

Dosed Tracker is a personal medication reminder and tracking tool. **We do not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations.** The App is intended solely to help you manage your personal medication schedule.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you use Dosed Tracker, you may provide the following information:

- **Personal Profile:** Your first name or nickname (optional), used to personalize reminder notifications.
- **Medication Data:** Medication names, dosages, schedules, times, and any notes you choose to enter about your medications.
- **Reminder Preferences:** Your preferred notification times and scheduling preferences.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Device Information:** We may collect limited device information (e.g., device type, operating system version) to ensure the App functions correctly and to diagnose technical issues.
- **Usage Data:** Anonymous usage statistics (e.g., feature usage, crash logs) to help us improve the App.
- **Push Notification Tokens:** Device tokens required to deliver local push notifications for medication reminders.

### 2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

- We do **not** collect your real name, email address, phone number, or physical address unless you explicitly provide it.
- We do **not** collect health information beyond what you voluntarily enter for medication tracking.
- We do **not** track your precise location.
- We do **not** sell your personal information to third parties.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide core App functionality (medication tracking, reminders, and scheduling).
- Personalize your experience (e.g., using your name in reminder messages).
- Send you local push notifications at the times you configure.
- Improve and optimize App performance and reliability.
- Diagnose and fix technical issues.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Local Storage

The majority of your data—including medication names, dosages, schedules, and reminder settings—is stored **locally on your device** using secure, encrypted storage mechanisms (Expo SecureStore and AsyncStorage). This data is not transmitted to our servers unless you explicitly enable cloud backup or synchronization features.

### 4.2 Backend Services

The App communicates with a backend service for non-critical features (e.g., generating personalized reminder messages). When this occurs:
- Only your first name and medication name are transmitted.
- No personally identifiable health information is sent.
- All communication is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.

### 4.3 Security Measures

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:
- Encrypted data transmission (TLS/HTTPS).
- Secure local storage on your device.
- Minimal data collection practices.

**Important:** While we take steps to protect your data, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share limited data only in the following circumstances:

### 5.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

### 6.1 Access and Control

You have full control over your data within the App:
- **View:** All medication and reminder data is visible within the App interface.
- **Edit:** You can modify or update your medication information at any time.
- **Delete:** You can delete individual medications or reset all App data through the Settings screen.

### 6.2 Notification Preferences

You can enable or disable push notifications at any time through:
- The App’s Settings screen
- Your device’s system notification settings (iOS Settings → Notifications → Dosed Tracker)

### 6.3 Data Deletion

To delete all your data from the App:
1. Go to **Settings** within the App
2. Tap **Reset All Data**
3. Confirm the deletion

This will remove all locally stored medication data, schedules, and preferences. Note that if you have enabled any cloud backup, you must separately delete data from that service.

## 7. Children’s Privacy

Dosed Tracker is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete the information.

## 8. Health Disclaimer

**Dosed Tracker is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.** The App is designed solely as a personal reminder tool. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider:
- Before starting, stopping, or changing any medication.
- If you miss a dose or experience side effects.
- For any medical questions or concerns.

**Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking it because of information you accessed through this App.**
